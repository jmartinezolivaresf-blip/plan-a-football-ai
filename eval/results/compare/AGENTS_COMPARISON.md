# Foundry agents (gpt-4.1-mini) against the rule agents: E1 and E3

Generated by `python -m eval.compare_agents --report-only` from `eval/results/compare/rows.jsonl`; no number is typed by hand. Everything is measured inside our simulator on synthetic data.

## Design (declared before the run)

- Units: pre-match workflows of both staffs. E1: official test seasons 8001-8005 and fresh seasons 8006-8015 (random trait kinds, never used before), three matchups each; E3: the official match season 8101, six matchups and their seeds. Both systems ran every unit with the same seasons, matchups, seeds and the scoring functions of `eval/run.py`; the rule agents reproduce the committed E1/E3 exactly.
- The model (gpt-4.1-mini, Global Standard, Microsoft Foundry) ran 2 times per unit with the production prompts and validators; nothing was tuned for this comparison. Rule agents: once (deterministic). Validation failures and template fallbacks count as the system's output.
- E1: precision and recall of the weaknesses a scouting report flags against the hidden traits. E3: share of chosen plans with a lever that attacks a real hidden weakness (the random-plan baseline is the share of all lever combinations that would also count).
- Differences are model minus rules, with 95% paired bootstrap intervals over units (10000 resamples, fixed seed); "significant" means the 95% interval excludes zero. The model's runs are pooled per unit.

## Where the gap comes from (diagnostic over every unit; not a second comparison)

- Weaknesses flagged per scouting report: rules 2.61, model 1.17.
- The model's 122 reports that passed validation: precision 0.806, recall 0.317: it is precise but flags few weaknesses, so it misses most real ones.
- The model's 161 plans that were not the template attack a real weakness 0.559 of the time, against 0.547 for a random plan on the same opponents.

## Limitations

- A synthetic world: these numbers say how each system does inside our simulator, not in real football.
- One model, its default sampling and the production prompts; a different model or prompt could do better or worse. E3 counts a lever-trait match only, not whether the plan would win.
- The official E3 set has 12 plans: its interval is wide; the pooled set adds every plan of the E1 units under the same criterion.
